01 — The Identity

MORE THAN A FAMILY.
A LEGACY.

The Shinshiki Clan is not simply a family connected through blood relationships. It is an extraordinary family unit built around a shared identity, a distinctive philosophy, mutual respect, and unbreakable unity.

Emerging near the beginning of the 21st century, this remarkable clan represents a unique combination of historical dignity and modern practicality. Its members value intelligence, courage, innovation, independence, determination, and humanity.

These details form part of the clan's fictional identity and narrative — a mythology through which the values behind this portfolio are expressed.

02 — The Archive

THE LEGACY WRITTEN IN STONE

The clan's history began with the renaming of its first Shinshiki member. Its true origins remain secret, preserved through ancient inscriptions engraved on thin square stone tablets — each roughly one inch thick — kept inside the sacred Shinshiki Shrine. Traditionally, access to the shrine is reserved for those born with the Shinshiki name.

— touch the stone to reveal the inscription —

"Here begins the name. Here rests the origin. What came before remains in silence, and the silence belongs to the Shinshiki."

— inscription of the First Shinshiki

  1. Everything began with a renaming. The first Shinshiki member accepted the name that would define a lineage — not merely a label, but an identity to be carried, protected, and elevated by every generation that followed.

  2. The true origins of the clan were carved into thin square stone tablets, each approximately one inch thick. These inscriptions rest within the sacred Shinshiki Shrine, whose doors open only to those born with the Shinshiki name.

  3. Generation after generation, the lineage continued. Each era added its own chapter — new members, new achievements, new trials — while the core identity remained unchanged: intelligence, courage, and unity.

  4. As of 2026, the identity lives on through the 61st Shinshiki — a generation that bridges the clan's mysterious past with the technologies and questions of the modern world.

  5. An unwritten chapter. The future of the Shinshiki Clan remains open — shaped by the choices, creations, and character of those who carry the name next. This is a fictional narrative framework, ready for future entries.

THE HIERARCHY OF EXCELLENCE

The clan's internal recognition system is not determined by birth order alone. Every Shinshiki member may possess certain extraordinary qualities — but only rare individuals demonstrate a complete combination of the clan's most exceptional characteristics. Such members may receive special numerical recognition according to their abilities and qualities. The process is led by the current recognized expert and requires unity and agreement throughout the clan.

05 — The Mind

THE PHILOSOPHY OF SHINSHIKI

"Question Everything. Understand Existence. Accept Reality."

The Shinshiki philosophy draws inspiration from Buddhism while maintaining a close relationship with traditions such as Shinto. Its members explore fundamental questions concerning existence, life, death, identity, and the nature of reality. Select a teaching to open its full explanation.

These are the clan's philosophical viewpoints — presented as part of its fictional narrative, not as established scientific conclusions.

06 — The Bond

NO SHINSHIKI STANDS ALONE.

Although Shinshiki members may differ in personality, abilities, and opinions, they share a deep sense of respect, trust, loyalty, and affection. No member is abandoned. When one member faces difficulties, the others stand together to support them — they look after one another, offer assistance when necessary, and preserve the unity of the clan.

"Different Strengths. One Identity. Unbreakable Unity."
